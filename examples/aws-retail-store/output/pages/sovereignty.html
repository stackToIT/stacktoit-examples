What this page does not say

  • Legal conclusions remain external. The block provides technical evidence and gaps and does not certify legal compliance.
  • Sovereignty is not residency: a region of a service is a fact about the service, never a statement about the sovereignty of a workload.
  • There is no score, no percentage and no count in this block, and no dimension is compared with a requirement in this build.
  • Jurisdiction, operator access, open interfaces, data portability and application coupling are not assessed, and every field of the exitability model but the Terraform coupling is not assessed.
  • Dependencies are the units the knowledge base maps to a STACKIT service, the source providers the knowledge base holds no schema of and the source types it does not know. A dependency the draft introduces without a source unit is not listed, and a unit with no STACKIT resource is not listed as removed.

The same block, with the pointers of its evidence, is in assurance/sovereignty.json, and in words in assurance/sovereignty.md.
