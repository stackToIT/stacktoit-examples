secret-iam-access · warning · 2 units, 2 records

The secret has grants for individual principals. Access on STACKIT Secrets Manager is coarser than a Secret Manager grant: a Secrets Manager user is read-only or read-write for the whole secrets engine of its instance, with no scope to one secret or path. Narrower access exists only as role bindings on the instance or on a secret group (a named list of secrets) in the alpha API of the service, and the pinned provider offers them as resources of its experimental IAM feature (stackit_secretsmanager_instance_role_binding_v1 and stackit_secretsmanager_secret_group_role_binding_v1), which its documentation says may change or be removed; the specification does not list the roles a secret group accepts. A grant of one Google principal on one secret therefore cannot be carried one to one: decide per principal whether it gets a user of the whole instance, a role binding on a secret group of the secrets it may read, or a separate Secrets Manager instance.

Evidence: https://github.com/stackitcloud/stackit-api-specifications/blob/main/services/secrets-manager/v1/secrets-manager.json, https://github.com/stackitcloud/stackit-api-specifications/blob/main/services/secrets-manager/v1alpha/secrets-manager.json, https://registry.terraform.io/providers/stackitcloud/stackit/0.121.0/docs/resources/secretsmanager_instance_role_binding_v1, https://registry.terraform.io/providers/stackitcloud/stackit/0.121.0/docs/resources/secretsmanager_secret_group_role_binding_v1, https://registry.terraform.io/providers/stackitcloud/stackit/0.121.0/docs/resources/secretsmanager_user

The two ledgers

Two files in this report folder hold the rows behind the statements of this workspace as a spreadsheet. A row never states more than the assessment, the plans or the knowledge base state.

  • assurance/equivalence-ledger.csv: the equivalence ledger, 201 rows, one per unit and semantic that the source states and one per unit and property for the properties the draft does not carry, no semantic accounts for or that are critical and still open.
  • assurance/evidence-ledger.csv: the evidence ledger, 72 rows, one per document the other blocks cite, with the date it was checked and the date after which it is to be checked again. Evidence that states no such dates is undated, never current.

Limits of the equivalence ledger:

  • Grain: one row per source unit and semantic of the assessment that the source states or may state (UNIT_SEMANTIC), plus one row per source unit and property for each of three lists the assessment names: properties the draft does not carry, properties no semantic accounts for, and critical properties still open (UNIT_PROPERTY). A property named by more than one list has one row per list. A row for every source property is not possible yet: the assessment does not record which properties a generator reads.
  • Vocabulary v1: the disposition column prints the words the contracts and the property accounting use today. LEDGER means the contract hands the semantic to the semantic ledger of the draft; PRESERVED and TRANSFORMED appear only where the contract result or the draft's plan ledger states them.
  • intent_id, target_capability and target_resource are stated only for a semantic that a family written from a plan (security groups, routing tables) carries through the plan's own ledger; for every other row they are not recorded.
  • target_effective_behavior is NOT_ASSESSED on every row: the behaviour of the target is evidenced per field of a plan resource (units[].behavior_evidence), and a semantic is not a field.
  • residual_risk, required_action and decision_id are stated only for a critical semantic that is open; they are the sentences and the record that the Decisions section already shows, quoted whole. A sentence that names a record internal to the product is not quoted, and its column is not recorded.
  • invalidated_by names the inputs of the whole run (manifest.json invalidation) that, when they change, invalidate the row; no row has an invalidation of its own.
  • evidence_refs name rows of evidence_ledger. Evidence that names a record internal to the product is not listed, and evidence that states no check date and no date to check it again is listed UNDATED, never CURRENT.
  • 504 semantics of the contracts that the source does not state (it observed no) are not listed: the source has nothing there to carry. The kernel claims of assessment.json count them the same way (absent_claims).
