Readiness by dimension

12 dimensions

Readiness is shown per dimension. There is no overall score, and no dimension here means that the estate is safe to migrate.

Three states answer three questions. Coverage says how much of what the dimension counts is covered (Covered: all of it); it never says that anything is decided, validated or ready to deploy. Migration decisions is read from the Blockers and Open decisions columns of the same row: a Covered dimension can have blockers. Target validation says what StackToIT’s own checks established about the generated target for that dimension; no STACKIT plan is run, so nothing here means the draft plans or applies.

Semantic Accounting counts the critical settings the assessment accounted for; Critical Resolution counts those that are decided: accounted is not resolved. Terraform Draft Accounting counts the units whose critical settings are accounted for in the draft; it does not say that the draft validates or plans against STACKIT.

The six dimensions of the first screen read the rows of this matrix: Analyzed reads Source Fidelity; Drafted reads Terraform Draft Accounting (the units whose critical settings are accounted for in the draft; Drafted counts the units that have a generated resource); Validated reads the Target validation column; Deployable reads the package state of Migration Execution; Application Ready reads Target Functional Fit and the Operational Readiness row (no application compatibility contract exists in this build); Equivalent reads Semantic Accounting and Critical Resolution (accounted for is not resolved, and resolved is not equivalent). Security, Sovereignty & Exitability and Cost Confidence have pages of their own and no user-first dimension.
